# Source access, provenance and integration matrix

**Engineering review:** 2026-11-08. **Catalog:** 2026.11.08.1. This preserves engineering findings from published provider documentation and controlled public HTTP retrievals. Under Mark's 2026-11-09 two-role amendment in `AGENTS.md`, **Engineering prepares source-access, privacy, licensing and redistribution evidence; independent Pulse QA reviews it**. The old GP-ENG-001 reference to Iris no longer routes any work to a third role. This historical source inventory is neither an independent current acceptance nor provider/license clearance; obligations and permitted live-use behavior still require exact, independent review.

## What is enabled

The installation has 14 enabled public source configurations, including USGS, NASA EONET, NWS, NOAA SWPC, GDACS, UK FCDO, ECB, Japan MOF, SAnews, Global Voices, India PIB, and three World Bank series. They require no API key. The 14 other catalog entries are disabled or manual; the application must not let a checkbox override `access_status=review_required`.

The app fetches public metadata, headlines, and bounded feed excerpts. It does not download complete publisher articles, photographs, account data, or video/audio files. Feed text is treated as untrusted plain text. Source attribution, author/byline where supplied, original URL, source-license text, publication date, retrieval date, and source update time travel with the record. A missing publication time remains explicitly unknown. An official statement confirms attribution, not all its claims. EONET and GDACS reuse external reporting and are not automatically independent corroboration.

Collection intervals are at least 10 minutes by default, 5 minutes only where configured and permissible, and 24 hours for annual World Bank series. Provider Retry-After and cache lifetimes can lengthen them. Failed sources are isolated, and no retry fabricates missed observations. Per-host throttles survive restart through stored rate-limit checkpoints.

## Evidence and dates

Primary provider references were read during engineering:

- [USGS GeoJSON](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php) explicitly describes a programmatic application feed and publishes event IDs, measurement timestamps, magnitude types, locations and update fields. The adapter uses the summary feed rather than repeated catalog searches. Magnitude-based importance is not a casualty estimate.
- [NASA EONET v3](https://eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/v3) identifies curated metadata and original sources. [Its disclaimer](https://eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov/what-is-eonet) applies. Catalog presence is not a quantified impact assessment, and no invented severity is assigned.
- [NWS API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api) documents open data, caching, rate limits, and application User-Agent requirements. Exercise/test alerts are filtered. Polygon/zone descriptions do not become fabricated incident coordinates.
- [GDACS overview](https://www.gdacs.org/About/overview.aspx) permits standard-feed integration. [GDACS terms](https://www.gdacs.org/About/termofuse.aspx) describe model outputs and require validation before consequential use. The RSS itself identifies its copyright field as public domain; third-party linked content is not copied.
- [GOV.UK terms](https://www.gov.uk/help/terms-conditions) describe OGL content and feed use. [ECB conditions](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html) require accurate attribution and identification of changes; authored research-paper exceptions are retained.
- [Japan MOF RSS](https://www.mof.go.jp/english/about_mof/rss/index.html) publishes its reader endpoint. [MOF copyright notice](https://www.mof.go.jp/english/about_mof/notice/index.html) applies PDL 1.0 with exclusions. No maps or agency logos are copied.
- [SAnews rights](https://www.sanews.gov.za/content/privacy-policy) permit credited media use and prohibit framing. The initially linked `rss-old.xml` was stale; the working `rss.xml` contains current November 8 publications. Health includes last retrieval and latest source publication independently.
- [Global Voices attribution policy](https://globalvoices.org/about/global-voices-attribution-policy/) permits credited text adaptation under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Preserve author and original link, identify abridgement/translation, and do not import separately owned images/audio/video.
- [PIB copyright](https://www.pib.gov.in/content/186_2_Copyright-Policy.aspx?lang=1&reg=5) permits accurate credited reproduction except third-party material. Its inspected RSS omits publication timestamps and currently contains Hindi; those limitations remain visible.
- [World Bank licensing](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses) sets CC BY 4.0 with additional terms and dataset-specific exceptions. [API documentation](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api-documentation) is the access contract. Country-year observations preserve units and revisions. `lastupdated` is dataset currency, never invented as an individual release date.

Controlled live checks on November 8 successfully parsed USGS (11 reports), EONET (37), NWS (79), SWPC (84), GDACS (286), FCDO (20), ECB (14), Japan MOF (100), SAnews (10), Global Voices (15), and PIB (20). World Bank returned 196 inflation observations, 196 unemployment observations, and 200 GDP observations across the configured countries/years. Counts are observations of those requests, not availability or completeness promises. GDACS initially returned 503, then succeeded; source outage behavior remains relevant. HTTP 200 was not treated as sufficient: two parser issues (HTTP source links / NWS fallback) were found and fixed before recording these parsed counts. No live content is bundled as a synthetic fixture.

The standalone source suite passes **41 focused tests**, including RSS/Atom/RDF/CAP parsing, XML entity rejection, script stripping, source metadata, private/ambiguous URL rejection, callback suppression while paused, no duplicate concurrent collection, bounded stop, persistent host throttles, Retry-After date/seconds, cache validators, stale-data disclosure, unavailable sources, and decompression-bomb limits. These are engineering tests, not independent release QA.

## Media and restricted sources

[YouTube's Tousi TV channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TousiTV) and [streams page](https://www.youtube.com/@TousiTV/streams) resolve with the Tousi TV channel title; [Tousi's own about page](https://tousi.tv/about) identifies the outlet. Automated live status is not configured. A channel/upload listing does not prove a broadcast is live, and Global Pulse therefore offers the manual channel link without claiming a live alert. User-selected videos may use the official embedded player if allowed by the uploader. No website reverse engineering, stream downloading, autoplay with sound, or quota bypass is used.

[ReliefWeb](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/) now requires an approved appname for read/search; none was supplied. [BBC terms](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/usingthebbc/bbc_terms_of_use_19September2022english.pdf) require permission for business RSS use. [UN terms](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/terms-of-use) restrict redistribution and derivatives. [EBC's current terms](https://www.ebc.com.br/termos-de-uso-e-condicoes-gerais-do-portal-da-ebc) have personal/noncommercial scope; older CC notices cannot silently override them. Those adapters remain disabled pending the stated review or approval.

Public camera entries link to [NPS air-quality cameras](https://www.nps.gov/features/ard/webcams/) and [coastal webcams](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/oceans/webcams.htm). The air-quality network describes delayed images rather than continuous live video. Radio entries point to [WebSDR](https://websdr.org/) and [KiwiSDR's public directory](https://kiwisdr.com/public/). Directory inclusion does not assert every receiver is currently online or grant recording/redistribution rights. The user opens a provider's receiver page, and the application performs no audio recording, retransmission, private-camera discovery, private-account collection, or precise individual/tactical tracking.

## Network and catalog boundaries

- Fetch domains and redirect destinations are an exact reviewed allowlist. Only HTTPS on port 443 is collected. Credentials in URLs, localhost/private/reserved addresses, numeric host ambiguities and local suffixes are rejected. User feeds additionally require public DNS answers; a DNS-blocked environment fails closed. Reviewed built-in domains use the normal platform/enterprise resolver; the application does not claim to defeat hostile DNS or a malicious configured proxy.
- Each decoded body and wire body is limited to 6 MiB, XML entity resolution is disabled, HTML/script content is reduced to bounded text, and at most 600 report entries are parsed from a response. Gzip/deflate expansion is bounded before allocation; unsupported encodings are rejected. Worker count is at most four with paced starts and host throttling.
- Catalog data cannot execute Python. Remote catalog updates are disabled. Catalog changes ship with the reviewed application package; changing source rights or domains requires a new source review.
- No confidential credentials are distributed. Optional providers remain catalog entries/manual links until the requisite supported access configuration and licensing review exist.
